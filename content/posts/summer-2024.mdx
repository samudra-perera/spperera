Three themes for the summer: health and wellness, programming, and hobbies. At the end of
it, I'll come back to this post and see how close I got.

## Health and wellness

### Physical health

Since getting back into school my physical health has been on a downward trend — no one's
fault but mine. I've been making consistent excuses to skip physical activity: diet, water
intake, sleep, all neglected. I went from 165lb at the start of 2023 to 187lb by February
2024. The goal is back under 165lb; as of writing this I'm at 176.4lb.

Weight is only one metric of "healthy," though. Two other areas I want to improve are
cardiovascular health and my footy grind — running at least once a week (minimum 5km) and
playing soccer at least twice a week. Might be ambitious. We'll see.

### Mental health

The big one is [good, consistent sleep](https://www.columbiapsychiatry.org/news/how-sleep-deprivation-affects-your-mental-health).
A solid 8+ hours of uninterrupted sleep has me feeling like a superhero the next day. Sleep
is the foundation for the day — a bad foundation makes for a bad day.

## Programming goals

Three goals here. First, another hackathon — UofT's Climate Hacks was a blast and I want
that environment again. Second, build an end-to-end AI model, either through school or
through [Spingle](https://spingle.ai/). Third, a small project over the summer — shortlist
so far is a collision simulator or an NFT-based Tamagotchi.

## Hobbies

Short and sweet: get back into creativity. I've been thinking about ways to blend creative
ideas with technology and kill two birds with one stone. Other than that, I just need to
actually do it.

I also want to write consistently on this site about a variety of topics — constant days of
writing code has dulled my writing muscle. And reading five books would be great.
