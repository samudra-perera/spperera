## Health and wellness

### Physical health

The cardio and soccer goals held up, and then some — full numbers, including a year of
WHOOP data, are in [the year in review](/writing/year-in-review).

The 2026 goal isn't "sleep more." It's a number: fewer than 30 nights after 2am, down
from 208. The trend ran the wrong way all last year — 29% of nights after 2am in
September, climbing to 81% by December — and [the full derivation is
here](/writing/year-in-review): modeling the redistribution moves average recovery from
58.2% to 64.8%. One hard cap on bedtime, not a vague intention.

### Mental health

The same data shows sustained hard-training streaks — three or more consecutive days at
strain 14+ — crater recovery and REM specifically, the sleep stage most tied to mood.
[Full breakdown here](/writing/year-in-review). The practical rule: never three hard
days back to back without a real rest day between.
