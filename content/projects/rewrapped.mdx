Spotify already has collaborative playlists — but not a playlist generator that builds a
group playlist from everyone's individual listening history. ReWrapped does that: point it
at a group, and it generates a shared playlist weighted by what the group actually listens
to.

## Tech stack

Frontend: React, Material UI, Jest.
Backend: Node, Express, PostgreSQL.
CI: SonarQube for static analysis.

## Results

Built by a team of six under strict agile practice — two-week sprints, with a strong
emphasis on shipping thoroughly tested, robust code over moving fast.
