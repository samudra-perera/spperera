## How it works

Transparence gives current and past employees a simple interface to request verification
of employment and achievements from their employers. When an employer confirms a request,
an NFT is minted and sent to the requesting user's wallet — a portable, tamper-evident
credential they can present on résumés and social platforms.

## Results

Among 900+ participants and 160+ teams, Transparence won Best Use Case, with a $1,700 prize
attached.
